# Product Brief — Aether Phone 1 (Ultra Titanium) Flagship Showcase

## 1. Product Discovery (Layer 01)
- **Product Name**: Aether Phone 1 (Titanium Edition)
- **Target Audience**: Tech connoisseurs, hardware enthusiasts, designers, engineers, and premium buyers who appreciate transparent industrial design and extreme engineering.
- **Core Value Proposition**: An uncompromised flagship smartphone combining aerospace-grade brushed titanium unibody, 2nm Quantum Neural Silicon, and a modular periscope camera system — presented through an **Interactive 3D Exploded Anatomy Experience**.
- **JTBD**:
  > *When I evaluate a premium smartphone, I want to inspect its internal engineering, thermal architecture, and sensor hardware through an authentic exploded 3D model, so that I can understand the craftsmanship and justify the investment.*

---

## 2. Product Strategy & Principles (Layer 02)
- **Visual Archetype**: **Archetype 1 (Skeuomorphic Hardware Precision)** + **Archetype 4 (Obsidian Minimalist Luxury)**.
- **Key Tenet**: "Every millimeter of internal volume is engineered with purpose — make the invisible engineering tangible."
- **Interactive Focus**:
  1. **Hero 3D Flagship Presentation** with dynamic light reflections.
  2. **Interactive 3D Exploded Anatomy Workbench** with 6 focus hotspots (Ceramic Glass, Tandem OLED, 2nm SoC, Vapor Chamber Cooling, Periscope Optical Array, Titanium Unibody).
  3. **Interactive Finish Switcher** (Space Titanium, Stealth Obsidian, Radiant Silver).
  4. **Performance Benchmark Comparator** (Ray Tracing FPS, Thermal Headroom, Battery Endurance).

---

## 3. Interaction Design Decisions (DDR)
- **DDR-001 (Exploded Anatomy Hotspots vs Flat Specs)**:
  - Users explore components spatially with interactive glowing pulse pins that reveal micro-spec tooltips and zoom anchors, rather than reading a static spec sheet.
- **DDR-002 (Layer Deconstruction Slider)**:
  - An interactive disassembly slider allows users to peel back layers from the finished unibody down to raw silicon.
