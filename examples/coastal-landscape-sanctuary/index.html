Ecology & Flora

Posidonia Oceanica (海王草甸)

The turquoise clarity of this cove is preserved by ancient underwater meadows of Posidonia Oceanica. These marine plants oxygenate the shallows, buffer the swell energy before it reaches the beach, and foster rich nurseries of sea bream and silver wrasse.

Tidal Physics

Micro-Tides of the Mediterranean (地中海微潮)

Unlike the Atlantic, Mediterranean shores experience semi-diurnal tides with modest amplitudes of 30 to 45 centimeters. Wind pressure (the Mistral and Scirocco) often exerts a greater influence on water height than lunar gravitational pull, turning every afternoon into an intimate dialogue between breeze and stone.

Architecture

The Cliffside Sanctuary Metaphor (临海庇护所)

Built with local calcareous limestone and exposed cedar beams, the structure dissolves the boundary between domestic shelter and raw ocean horizon. Salt-tolerant succulents, olive trees, and sea lavender root into the stone crevices, scenting the evening air with honey and brine.

Sensory Ritual

Bioluminescent Midnight (夜光藻微明)

During warm summer lulls, single-celled dinoflagellates Noctiluca scintillans gather in quiet eddies. When disturbed by the curling wash of a wave or the dip of an oar, mechanical stress triggers luciferase enzymes, producing cold, electric cyan bioluminescence across the surf.
